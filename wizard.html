What should it read?

Every button below plays this. Pick something long enough to judge by.

1 · How big is it?

a longer throat and a lower voice box — a mouse and a giant are the same tube, different sizes

2 · How lively is it?

how far the pitch and the loudness move. flat is what a robot sounds like

3 · What is the voice like?

breathy, clear, rough or pressed — how the vocal folds themselves behave

4 · How does it come out?

the consonants rather than the voice — how hard they hiss and pop, how fast the voice comes back after a closure, how much the walls swallow

5 · How fast does it talk?

seconds per sound. real speech is about 70 to 80 milliseconds

6 · Two at a time

the four answers get you close; this walks from there. play both, keep the one you like, and it walks again from the winner

Keep it

A seed is the whole voice — throat length, pitch, liveliness, texture, speed. Paste it into the Lab to keep tuning, or send it to someone.
